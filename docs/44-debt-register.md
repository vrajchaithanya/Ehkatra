| TD-12 | `Decimal` is a 128-bit scaled integer, not IEEE 754-2008 decimal128 (ADR-035) | Full IEEE decimal128 is thousands of lines plus a conformance suite, in a `no_std` kernel with a 5-crate budget; the available crates offer *fewer* digits (~28) than either | No bit-level interchange with other decimal128 implementations; no ±Inf/NaN; no quantum. | TD-14 | Lookup is exact-match only; approximate match returns `#N/A` (D-044) | Excel's approximate match is correct only on a sorted key, and v0.1 cannot verify sortedness. | TD-16 | Range-to-scalar collapse takes the top-left cell, not Excel's implicit intersection (`@`) | Implicit intersection needs the *calling cell's* row and column, which arrives with the dependency graph | A formula like `=A1:A10+1` in a scalar context gives Excel's answer only when the caller sits on the first row | Row 7, when the dep graph supplies the evaluating cell's position |
